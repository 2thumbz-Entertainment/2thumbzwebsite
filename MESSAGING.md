## 1. The one-line positioning

> **2ThumbZ is the collegiate IP partner for business.**
> We hold the rights to 300+ schools, run the licensing and compliance machinery,
> and own the distribution channels — so any company can **license** our content,
> **distribute** through our channels, or have us **build** the product for them.

**Why this works:** it states what we own (rights + compliance + channels) and
immediately frames the three ways to engage. It never implies we sell to fans.

### Elevator version (≤25 words)
We aggregate officially licensed content from 300+ colleges and give businesses
three ways to use it: license the IP, launch through our channels, or have us
build the product.

## 2. Who we target (and who we don't)

### We sell to businesses. Full stop.
Primary buyers — anyone who wants collegiate brand equity in a commercial product:

- **Product & platform companies** — messaging, gaming, avatar/social, music/audio,
  fintech, sports-tech, wallpaper/personalization
- **Brands & CPG/retail** — companies wanting licensed collegiate tie-ins
- **Studios & publishers** — game studios, app developers, media companies
- **Agencies** — building something on a client's behalf
- **Universities & rights-holders** — the *supply* side (see "For Universities");
  a distinct audience, not a buyer of our solutions

### We do NOT target consumers.
Fans discover our finished apps in the App Store, Galaxy Store, Spotify, etc.
The website never asks a fan to download, buy, or browse-to-purchase. If a sentence
would make sense to a college student shopping for a ringtone, **it does not belong
on this site.**

**Anti-audience test:** Before publishing any line, ask *"Is the reader a company
deciding whether to work with us, or a fan deciding whether to buy something?"*
If it's the fan — rewrite it.

## 3. The three offerings — License / Distribute / Build

These are the spine of the site. Home introduces all three; each gets a dedicated page.

### 3.1 LICENSE — *Put our IP in your product*
**For:** companies that have a product and want to add officially licensed
collegiate content to it. They build; we supply.

- **Headline:** Plug 300+ schools into your product.
- **Subhead:** License officially licensed collegiate content — stickers, audio,
  wearables, graphics, in-game items — through one agreement. You build the
  experience; we handle every school approval and royalty.
- **Core value props:**
  - One agreement = 300+ schools (vs. negotiating each individually)
  - 5,000+ production-ready assets, in the formats your product needs
  - We own the compliance: approvals, brand guidelines, royalty reporting
  - 20+ years of collegiate licensing relationships (IMG/CLC, Fanatics, direct)
- **Primary objection handled:** "Licensing collegiate IP is slow and fragmented."
  → We've already aggregated it; one relationship covers everything.
- **CTA:** *Start a Licensing Conversation →*

### 3.2 DISTRIBUTE — *Reach fans we already reach*
**For:** companies that want collegiate IP **and** want to reach the college-fan
audience through channels they don't currently own. We extend their reach.

- **Headline:** Reach 180M+ college fans through channels we already own.
- **Subhead:** Enhance your product with collegiate IP and launch it through our
  established distribution — Apple App Store, Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store,
  Amazon Appstore, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and Meta — to reach
  customers you aren't reaching today.
- **Core value props:**
  - Live, proven distribution footprint across major stores and platforms
  - Access to a passionate, hard-to-reach 180M+ college-fan audience
  - Skip the cost and time of building store presence and audience from scratch
  - Co-marketing into a fan base that already trusts officially licensed content
- **Primary objection handled:** "We have a product but can't reach this audience."
  → Our channels and audience are already built; plug in.
- **CTA:** *Explore a Distribution Partnership →*

### 3.3 BUILD — *We build it for you*
**For:** companies that want a finished collegiate product but don't want to build
it themselves. Turnkey product development on top of our IP.

- **Headline:** Have us build it. Licensed, compliant, shipped.
- **Subhead:** We design, develop, and ship branded collegiate products using our
  IP and 20+ years building exactly this — from ringtones and apps to wearables
  and in-game content. You get a finished product; we own the rights and the work.
- **Core value props:**
  - Full-service: concept → design → development → launch → distribution
  - Built on IP we already hold, so there's no licensing gap to close
  - 20+ years of shipping collegiate digital products (this is our origin and edge)
  - Optionally combine with DISTRIBUTE to launch through our channels
- **Primary objection handled:** "We want this but don't have the team or the rights."
  → We have both. Tell us the product; we'll build and license it.
- **CTA:** *Tell Us What to Build →*

> **Note on the heritage story:** "From ringtones to..." is no longer a liability.
> On the BUILD page (and About) it is *proof* that we have shipped real collegiate
> products for decades. Frame it as a credential, never as our current business model.

## 6. Voice & tone

- **Confident, plain, B2B.** We're the experts who removed the hard part.
- **Specific over hype.** "300+ schools, one agreement" beats "endless possibilities."
- **Partner-oriented "you."** "You" = the business reading the page, never a fan.
- **Lead with the buyer's problem, resolve with our capability.**
- Short sentences. Active voice. Real numbers.

## 7. Do / Don't word list

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|---|---|
| partner, license, integrate, distribution channel | download, buy now, add to cart |
| businesses, companies, brands, studios, platforms | fans (as the reader/customer) |
| our IP / our library / our content / our channels | our app (as the thing we sell to you) |
| "your product," "your users," "your customers" | "your favorite team," "express your fandom" |
| "browse the library to license" | "browse / shop the catalog" |
| asset, IP, collection (as licensable inventory) | "Ringtone Collection," "Fan Engagement App" (as finished consumer SKUs) |
| "we build products for businesses" | "we make apps for fans" |

**The catalog rule:** the Content Library page lists *what you can license*, not
*what a fan can download*. Every item is an IP/asset category, not a shippable
consumer product with a price.
