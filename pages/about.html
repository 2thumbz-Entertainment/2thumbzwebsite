About 2ThumbZ

The turnkey collegiate content partner for major digital platforms.

Our Story

From Ringtones to Turnkey Content Partner

2ThumbZ was founded with a simple idea: college fans are passionate about their schools, and they deserve high-quality, officially licensed ways to express that fandom in the digital world.

What started as a collegiate ringtone provider has grown into the turnkey collegiate content partner for major digital platforms. Today, we hold licenses from 300+ schools through relationships with IMG/CLC, Fanatics, and direct partnerships — and we publish content across Apple, Samsung, Meta, Spotify, Amazon, and more.

Our mission: making it simple for major platforms to access collegiate content without negotiating with every school individually.
