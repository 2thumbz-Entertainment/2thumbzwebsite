Our Story

From Ringtones to Digital Content Leader

2ThumbZ was founded with a simple idea: college fans are passionate about their schools, and they deserve high-quality, officially licensed ways to express that fandom in the digital world.

What started as a collegiate ringtone provider has grown into the leading digital content licensor for the entire collegiate ecosystem. Today, we serve developers, platforms, game studios, and universities with content spanning mobile apps, stickers, audio, graphics, and digital wearables.

Our mission remains the same: connecting passionate college fans with the brands they love through innovative, officially licensed digital content.

Our Journey
