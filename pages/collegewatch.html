CollegeWatch

Officially licensed collegiate faces and gameday complications for Apple Watch — designed and built by 2ThumbZ. Launching for the 2026 season.

Demonstrates · Build + License

A licensed product, start to finish.

CollegeWatch puts fans' schools on their wrist — officially licensed collegiate faces and gameday complications for Apple Watch, with live scores, kickoff countdowns, stadium weather, and fight-song alarms. We own every step: concept, design, school-by-school licensing approval, development, and App Store release. Launching for the 2026 season.

It isn't a first attempt. We already operate a shipped watch-face business — 49 officially licensed collegiate faces live on Google Play for Wear OS and Samsung Galaxy Watch, across roughly 26 schools — plus 69 live collegiate apps on the Apple App Store. CollegeWatch brings that catalog to Apple Watch.

It's the clearest proof of what our Build model does: a partner brings the idea (or the audience), and we ship a finished, fully licensed collegiate product on top of the IP we already hold.
