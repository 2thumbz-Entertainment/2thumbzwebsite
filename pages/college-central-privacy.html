College Central Live Themes Help & Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

College Central Live Themes for iPhone and Android, published by 2ThumbZ, Inc.

Effective: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what the College Central Live Themes app ("College Central" or "the app") does with information, and covers the app together with its keyboard and its sticker app in Messages. It applies to College Central in place of the general 2ThumbZ privacy policy.

  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no data sales.
  • The keyboard never records, stores or sends what you type.
  • Purchases go through Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Your school choice and the wallpapers, keyboards and stickers you save stay on your phone.

1. What the app sends, and why

Subscriptions (Premium)

College Central uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, to confirm what you have bought and to restore it on a new phone. When you buy, restore or open the app, RevenueCat receives Apple's or Google's record of your purchase (the product, purchase and renewal dates, and transaction numbers) together with a random ID the app creates the first time you open it. That ID is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account or Google Account. RevenueCat also receives the app version, your phone's operating system version and model, your language and store country, and on iPhone the "identifier for vendor", a code Apple gives our apps on your phone that helps RevenueCat recognize the same phone. It sees your IP address as part of the connection. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Premium downloads

When a Premium subscriber saves a premium wallpaper, keyboard or sticker, the app sends its random ID to our server, which asks RevenueCat whether the subscription is active and, if it is, returns a pass that is valid for up to one hour. The app adds that pass to its premium downloads. Our server does not keep the ID or the pass.

Downloading content

The app downloads its catalog, wallpapers, keyboards and stickers from our content servers, which are hosted by Cloudflare. As with any web request, your phone's IP address and basic information about the app and operating system reach Cloudflare so the files can be delivered. We do not use this to identify you. Cloudflare may keep short-term logs for security and reliability under the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Email to support

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

2. What stays on your phone

  • The school you picked.
  • The wallpapers, keyboard skins and stickers you save, and whether Premium is active, which the keyboard and the sticker app read so they can show your premium items.
  • On iPhone, wallpapers are saved to Photos as Live Photos. The app asks only for permission to add photos: it writes your wallpaper and never looks at your photo library.

Deleting the app removes all of this.

3. What the app does not collect

  • What you type. On iPhone the keyboard works without "Allow Full Access", so iOS gives it no network access at all. On Android it does not record, store or send your typing either.
  • Your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your location.
  • Your photos, other than writing the wallpaper you chose to save.
  • The advertising identifier, analytics, usage tracking or crash-reporting data. The app contains no advertising or analytics software and never asks to track you.

If you have allowed Apple or Google to share app analytics and crash reports with developers, they send us anonymous, aggregated reports under their own privacy policies.

4. Who we share information with

Only the services above, and only to run the app: Apple or Google (purchases), RevenueCat (subscriptions) and Cloudflare (hosting). We do not sell or rent information, share it for advertising, or combine it with information from other companies.

5. How long information is kept

Purchase records are kept for as long as you could restore a purchase, and as long as tax and accounting rules require. Premium passes last at most an hour and are not stored on our server. Support email is kept as long as it is useful for helping you.

6. Children

College Central is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Purchases require an Apple Account or Google Account, which Apple and Google age-gate. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Manage or cancel Premium in your Apple Account subscriptions (iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store, then your profile, then Payments & subscriptions).
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stores on your phone.
  • To have your subscription record deleted, follow the steps on Delete your data: email us the order number from your purchase receipt. Deleting the record does not cancel a subscription, and tapping Restore purchases later asks Apple or Google for your purchases again.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information. Email us to use them; we answer within 30 days.

8. Licensed marks

College Central shows college and university trademarks and logos under license from their owners. Showing them does not send any information about you to us or to the schools.

9. Security

Everything the app sends and receives travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Premium files can be downloaded only with a valid pass.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. If a change is material, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
