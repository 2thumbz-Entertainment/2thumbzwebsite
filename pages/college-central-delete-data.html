College Central Live Themes Help & Support Privacy

Delete your data

College Central Live Themes, published by 2ThumbZ. The app has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to or close. This page explains how to ask us to delete the little we keep, and what happens to it.

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. Email my2thumbz@gmail.com with the subject "College Central data deletion".
  2. Include the order number from your purchase receipt, so we can find your record: on Android it starts with GPA. (in your Google Play order history or the receipt email); on iPhone it is the order ID on Apple's receipt email.
  3. We delete the record and confirm by email within 30 days.

Cancel first if you subscribe. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in Google Play (Play Store, your profile, Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple Account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions).

What we delete

  • Your subscription record held by RevenueCat, our subscription service: the app's random anonymous ID, your purchase history and the subscription's status and dates.
  • Any emails you sent us for support.

What is kept

  • Google and Apple keep their own records of your purchases, under their own privacy policies. We cannot delete those.
  • Totals in our sales reports that cannot be linked to you.
  • If you tap Restore purchases in the app after deletion, the app asks Google or Apple for your purchases again, which creates a new subscription record.

Data on your phone

The school you picked and the wallpapers, keyboards and stickers you saved stay on your phone. Uninstall College Central to remove them. On iPhone, wallpapers you saved to Photos stay there until you delete them.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
